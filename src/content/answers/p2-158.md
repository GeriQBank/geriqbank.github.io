## Answer at a glance

## Definition

A group of disorders affecting the lung interstitium and alveolar walls, with inflammation, fibrosis or both.

## Classification

- **Known cause:**
  - Drugs: amiodarone, nitrofurantoin, methotrexate, bleomycin, checkpoint inhibitors.
  - Occupational: asbestosis, silicosis.
  - Hypersensitivity pneumonitis (birds, moulds).
  - Connective tissue disease: RA, systemic sclerosis, myositis, Sjögren’s.
  - Radiation.
- **Idiopathic interstitial pneumonias:** IPF (the commonest in older adults), NSIP, organising pneumonia and others.
- **Granulomatous:** sarcoidosis.
- **Rare:** LAM, Langerhans cell histiocytosis, eosinophilic pneumonias.

## Pathogenesis of IPF

Repeated injury to alveolar epithelium in a susceptible host (ageing, short telomeres, MUC5B variants, smoking, reflux) leads to faulty repair, fibroblast activation and excess matrix.

## Clinical features

Progressive exertional breathlessness, dry cough, fine bibasal “Velcro” crackles, and clubbing in IPF. Look for connective tissue disease features. Pulmonary hypertension develops late.

## Investigations

- **Lung function:** a restrictive pattern with low DLCO; desaturation on the 6-minute walk.
- **HRCT is key.** A UIP pattern (subpleural, basal reticulation, honeycombing, traction bronchiectasis) diagnoses IPF in the right clinical setting without a biopsy. NSIP and hypersensitivity pneumonitis have their own patterns.
- **Bloods:** ANA, RF, anti-CCP, myositis panel, ENA, ANCA, and precipitins for hypersensitivity pneumonitis.
- BAL, cryobiopsy or surgical biopsy occasionally (rarely in older adults). A multidisciplinary discussion is the gold standard.
- Echo for pulmonary hypertension.

## Management

- Remove the exposure or drug; stop smoking.
- **IPF:** antifibrotics (nintedanib, pirfenidone) roughly halve the decline in FVC. Nintedanib is also used for other progressive fibrosis. Avoid the old steroid-azathioprine-NAC combination, which caused harm.
- **Other ILDs:** immunosuppression for CTD-ILD, hypersensitivity pneumonitis and NSIP (steroids, mycophenolate, rituximab).
- **Supportive care:** pulmonary rehabilitation, oxygen, vaccination, treatment of pulmonary hypertension, opioids for breathlessness, and palliative care. Transplant is rarely possible in older adults.
- **Acute exacerbations** carry high mortality; supportive care and steroids.

## Prognosis

Untreated IPF has a median survival of about 3–5 years.

## One-liner

Velcro crackles at the bases in an older smoker need an HRCT, not just a chest X-ray.
