## Definition (KDIGO)

Abnormal kidney structure or function for more than 3 months: eGFR below 60, or markers of damage such as albuminuria (ACR 30 mg/g or more).

- **Stages:** G1–G5 by eGFR (G3a 45–59, G3b 30–44, G4 15–29, G5 below 15), and A1–A3 by albuminuria.
- **In older adults:** eGFR falls with age. Use the race-free CKD-EPI 2021 equation, and add cystatin C when muscle mass is low.

## Causes

Diabetes (the commonest), hypertension, glomerulonephritis, polycystic kidneys, obstruction (BPH), renovascular disease, drugs (NSAIDs, lithium), and CKD of unknown cause in farming regions.

## Pathogenesis

Nephron loss leads to hyperfiltration in the remaining nephrons, then proteinuria, RAAS activation and fibrosis, and progressive loss.

## Complications

- Anaemia; mineral and bone disorder (high phosphate, low calcium, high PTH, vascular calcification).
- Acidosis, hyperkalaemia, fluid overload, hypertension.
- Cardiovascular disease, the leading cause of death.
- Uraemic symptoms (anorexia, itch, nausea, cognitive change), drug toxicity, malnutrition, frailty.

## Evaluation

eGFR and ACR, urine microscopy, renal ultrasound, electrolytes and bicarbonate, calcium, phosphate, PTH and vitamin D, blood count and iron, lipids, HbA1c. Do electrophoresis in older adults (myeloma).

## Slowing progression

- Blood pressure control, individualised in the frail.
- ACE inhibitor or ARB for albuminuria.
- **SGLT2 inhibitors** for eGFR 20 or above (DAPA-CKD, EMPA-KIDNEY).
- Finerenone for diabetic CKD with albuminuria; semaglutide in type 2 diabetes (FLOW).
- Statins.
- Avoid NSAIDs and unnecessary contrast, and adjust every drug dose.

## Treating complications

- Iron, then an ESA aiming for haemoglobin 10–11.5 g/dL.
- Phosphate binders, vitamin D and cinacalcet.
- Bicarbonate if below 22.
- Potassium binders; diuretics.
- Vaccination (hepatitis B, influenza, pneumococcal).

## Conservative kidney management in the elderly

For frail, multimorbid people over about 80 with stage 5 CKD, dialysis may add little life but many hospital days.

**Comprehensive conservative care** offers:

- Shared decisions.
- Symptom control (itch, fatigue, nausea, breathlessness).
- Anaemia and fluid management.
- Diet and potassium advice.
- Advance care planning and palliative care.

For those choosing dialysis, refer early and plan access (a fistula, or peritoneal dialysis).

## One-liner

For the frail very old with stage 5 CKD, conservative care is a real treatment, not a failure.
