## Why it matters

It causes falls, depression, cognitive decline (untreated vision loss is a dementia risk factor) and loss of independence.

## Age-related causes

- **Refractive error and presbyopia:** the commonest.
- **Cataract:** the leading cause of blindness in India.
- **Glaucoma:** open-angle is silent; angle-closure causes a painful red eye.
- Age-related macular degeneration (dry or wet), diabetic retinopathy, retinal vein and artery occlusions.
- GCA and ischaemic optic neuropathy (sudden loss); stroke (hemianopia).
- **Drugs:** hydroxychloroquine, ethambutol, amiodarone.

## Approach

- **History:** sudden or gradual, painful or painless, central or peripheral, any distortion.
- Visual acuity with pinhole: improvement means refractive error.
- Visual fields, pupils (RAPD), red reflex and fundoscopy, eye pressure, Amsler grid.
- For sudden loss: glucose, blood pressure and ESR.

## Urgent referral

Sudden loss, a painful red eye, or new distortion.

## Management

- Spectacles, cataract surgery, glaucoma drops, laser or surgery.
- Anti-VEGF injections for wet AMD; AREDS2 supplements for intermediate AMD.
- Laser or anti-VEGF for diabetic eye disease; steroids for GCA.
- **Low-vision aids:** magnifiers, lighting, contrast.
- Home safety and falls prevention; driving advice; regular eye checks, yearly in diabetes.

## One-liner

A bedside pinhole test tells you whether new glasses will fix it.
