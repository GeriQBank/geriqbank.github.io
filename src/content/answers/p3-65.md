## Answer at a glance

2. Organisms.

## Changing face

Most patients are now older, with degenerative valves, prosthetic valves or TAVI, pacemakers and ICDs, and healthcare-associated bacteraemia (lines, dialysis). Rheumatic heart disease persists in India.

## Organisms

- Staph aureus (commonest); viridans streptococci (dental source); enterococci (older men, GU source).
- **Streptococcus gallolyticus (bovis): look for colorectal cancer.**
- Coagulase-negative staph (prosthetic valves), HACEK, fungi.
- Culture-negative: prior antibiotics, Coxiella, Bartonella.

## In older adults it’s often subtle

Low or absent fever, fatigue, weight loss, confusion, falls, a murmur dismissed as “degenerative”, embolic stroke, heart failure. Peripheral stigmata are less common.

## Diagnosis (Duke-ISCVID 2023)

- **Major criteria:** typical organisms in blood cultures; imaging (vegetation, abscess, new regurgitation on echo; cardiac CT; PET/CT for prosthetic valves).
- **Minor criteria:** predisposition, fever, vascular or immunological phenomena, weaker microbiology.
- **Definite:** 2 major, 1 major plus 3 minor, or 5 minor.
- **Practical steps:** 3 sets of blood cultures before antibiotics; TTE first, then **TOE** if suspicion stays high, with prosthetic valves or devices, or for complications.

## Treatment

- Prolonged IV bactericidal antibiotics for 4–6 weeks. For enterococcus in the elderly, **ampicillin plus ceftriaxone** avoids gentamicin toxicity.
- Oral step-down in stable patients (POET).
- An endocarditis team.
- **Surgery** for heart failure from valve damage, uncontrolled infection (abscess, persistent bacteraemia, fungi) or large vegetations with emboli. Age raises surgical risk but isn’t a bar.
- Extract infected devices.

## Prevention

Dental hygiene; antibiotic prophylaxis before dental procedures only for the highest-risk patients (prosthetic valves, previous endocarditis); careful line care.

## One-liner

Fever, or just confusion, plus a murmur in an older patient means three blood cultures before antibiotics, and Strep gallolyticus means scope the colon.
