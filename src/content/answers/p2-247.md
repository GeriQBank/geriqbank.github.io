## Answer at a glance

1. Scale, and why older adults are different.
2. Sites and their features.
3. Diagnosis.
4. Treatment.
5. Cautions in older adults.

## Scale

About a fifth of notified TB in India. In older adults it is often atypical, disseminated or culture-negative, and diagnosed late. Reactivation is driven by immunosenescence, diabetes, steroids, CKD and malnutrition.

## Sites

- **Lymph nodes (commonest):** matted cervical nodes, cold abscess, sinuses.
- **Pleural:** lymphocytic exudate with high ADA.
- **Abdominal:** ileocaecal (pain, obstruction), or peritoneal with lymphocytic, high-ADA ascites.
- **Spinal (Pott’s):** back pain, gibbus, paraspinal abscess, cord compression. MRI shows adjacent vertebrae with disc destruction.
- **Genitourinary:** sterile pyuria, haematuria, small “thimble” bladder, epididymitis.
- **CNS:** TB meningitis (subacute headache, cranial nerve palsies, hydrocephalus, basal exudates), tuberculoma.
- **Miliary:** fever of unknown origin, hepatosplenomegaly, choroidal tubercles. “Cryptic” miliary TB is a known trap in the elderly.
- **Others:** pericardial (constriction), bone and joint, adrenal (Addison’s), skin, eye.

## Diagnosis

- **Get tissue or fluid:** smear, liquid culture, GeneXpert Ultra or Truenat (with rifampicin resistance), and histology for caseating granulomas.
- **Fluid ADA:** pleural, peritoneal, pericardial.
- **FNAC** for nodes.
- **Imaging:** CT, spinal MRI, and a chest X-ray to look for lung disease too.
- **TST and IGRA** can’t tell active from latent TB.

## Treatment (NTEP)

- The same 2HRZE plus 4HRE for most sites.
- **About 12 months for CNS and bone or spinal TB.**
- Steroids for TB meningitis and selected pericarditis.
- Surgery for cord compression, abscesses, constriction or obstruction.

## Older adults

Watch for liver toxicity and rifampicin interactions, dose for the kidneys, give pyridoxine, and support nutrition.

## One-liner

In older adults TB often hides outside the lungs, as a fever, a node, a backache or a confused patient, so get tissue, GeneXpert and a culture.
