## Why it matters

Common in older adults and a major cause of falls. In about a quarter, no cause is found (chronic idiopathic axonal polyneuropathy).

## Classification

- **Pattern:** length-dependent polyneuropathy, mononeuropathy, mononeuritis multiplex, polyradiculoneuropathy.
- **Fibre type:** large-fibre, small-fibre, motor, autonomic.
- **Pathology:** axonal or demyelinating.
- **Time course:** acute, subacute, chronic.

## Causes

- Diabetes (the commonest), alcohol.
- **B12 deficiency** (metformin, PPIs), hypothyroidism, CKD.
- **Drugs:**
  - Chemotherapy: vincristine, platinum drugs, taxanes, bortezomib.
  - Others: amiodarone, nitrofurantoin, isoniazid without pyridoxine, metronidazole, colchicine, linezolid.
- **Paraproteins:** MGUS, myeloma, amyloid.
- **Vasculitis** (mononeuritis multiplex).
- **Infections:** leprosy (important in India), HIV, hepatitis C.
- Paraneoplastic disease, CIDP, GBS (acute), copper deficiency, toxins (arsenic, lead), hereditary (CMT).

## Clinical features

- Numbness, tingling and burning in the feet (stocking-glove), imbalance, falls, distal weakness, cramps.
- Autonomic features: postural hypotension, gastroparesis, bladder problems.
- **Signs:** absent ankle reflexes; reduced vibration (some loss at the toes is normal with age), proprioception and pinprick; positive Romberg; distal wasting; foot deformity and ulcers.

## Investigations

- **First line:** glucose or HbA1c, B12 (with methylmalonic acid), TSH, renal and liver function, blood count, ESR and CRP, and **serum electrophoresis with immunofixation and free light chains**.
- **Second line:** HIV, hepatitis, ANA, ANCA, copper, anti-MAG and paraneoplastic antibodies, genetics.
- **Nerve conduction and EMG** to separate axonal from demyelinating.
- **Selected cases:** skin biopsy (small fibre), nerve biopsy (vasculitis), lumbar puncture (raised protein in CIDP), spinal MRI (myelopathy).

## Management

- **Treat the cause:**
  - Glucose control and B12 replacement.
  - Stop alcohol and culprit drugs.
  - IVIG, steroids or plasma exchange for CIDP.
  - Treat vasculitis; refer paraproteins to haematology.
- **Neuropathic pain:** duloxetine, pregabalin or gabapentin (dose for the kidneys, watch for falls), lidocaine or capsaicin patches. Avoid tricyclics and long-term opioids.
- **Falls prevention:** balance training, physiotherapy, good footwear, walking aids, home safety, ankle-foot orthoses for foot drop.
- **Foot care:** daily checks, podiatry, protective footwear.
- Treat autonomic symptoms such as postural hypotension.

## One-liner

In older adults, neuropathy is a falls diagnosis; find the treatable cause (diabetes, B12, paraprotein) and train the balance.
