## Late-onset hypogonadism

Symptoms (low libido, erectile dysfunction, fewer morning erections, fatigue, loss of muscle, low mood, hot flushes, anaemia, low bone density) **plus** consistently low testosterone. Testosterone falls about 1% a year after 40, but true symptomatic hypogonadism affects only about 2% of men aged 40–79.

## Diagnosis

- Two morning (7–11 am) fasting total testosterone levels below the laboratory range (roughly below 264–300 ng/dL). Calculate free testosterone if SHBG is abnormal.
- LH and FSH, to separate primary from secondary causes. If secondary: prolactin, iron studies, and pituitary MRI where indicated.
- First exclude reversible causes: obesity, diabetes, opioids, steroids, acute illness, sleep apnoea, alcohol.

## Evidence

- The Testosterone Trials (2016) showed modest gains in sexual function, mood, anaemia and bone density, but not in vitality or cognition.
- TRAVERSE (2023) found no excess of major cardiovascular events or prostate cancer, but more atrial fibrillation, AKI and pulmonary embolism.

## Contraindications

- Prostate or breast cancer.
- A prostate nodule, or PSA above 4 (above 3 if high-risk), without urological evaluation.
- Haematocrit above 50%.
- Untreated severe sleep apnoea, or severe LUTS.
- Uncontrolled heart failure, or MI or stroke in the past 6 months.
- Wanting to father children.

## Preparations

Daily gels; injections (enanthate every 1–2 weeks, with peaks and troughs and more erythrocytosis; or undecanoate every 10–14 weeks); patches; oral undecanoate.

## Monitoring

- Testosterone at 3–6 months, aiming for mid-normal.
- Haematocrit at baseline, 3–6 months, then yearly; stop or reduce above 54%.
- PSA and rectal exam at baseline and at 3–12 months.
- Bone density at 1–2 years if osteoporotic.
- Watch for oedema, sleep apnoea and gynaecomastia.

## Not recommended

For age-related low testosterone without symptoms, or for frailty or cognition alone.

## One-liner

Treat symptoms plus proven low testosterone, never a number alone, and watch the haematocrit.
