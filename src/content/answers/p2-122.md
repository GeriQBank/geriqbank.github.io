## Definition

An acute coronary syndrome with ischaemia at rest or minimal exertion but no myocardial necrosis (troponin not raised). It presents as rest angina over 20 minutes, new severe angina, or crescendo angina. With high-sensitivity troponin, it’s becoming rarer.

**Spectrum:** unstable angina, NSTEMI (troponin rise), STEMI.

## Pathophysiology

Plaque rupture or erosion with a non-occlusive thrombus, or a supply–demand mismatch.

## In older adults

Often atypical, presenting with breathlessness, fatigue, confusion, syncope, falls or epigastric pain; silent in diabetes.

## Evaluation

- ECG within 10 minutes: ST depression, T inversion, transient ST elevation.
- High-sensitivity troponin (0/1-hour or 0/2-hour pathways).
- GRACE and TIMI scores.
- Echo and bloods.
- Exclude aortic dissection, PE, pericarditis and reflux.

## Initial treatment

- **Aspirin** 150–300 mg loading dose, then 75–100 mg.
- **A P2Y12 inhibitor:** clopidogrel in older or high-bleeding-risk patients, ticagrelor otherwise. Avoid prasugrel at 75 and over.
- **Anticoagulation:** fondaparinux or enoxaparin, with doses adjusted for age and kidneys.
- **Nitrates for pain:** not with hypotension, PDE5 inhibitors or severe AS.
- A beta-blocker if safe, a high-intensity statin, an ACE inhibitor.
- Oxygen only if SpO₂ is below 90%.

## Invasive strategy

- Immediate for very high risk: instability, refractory pain, arrhythmia, acute heart failure.
- Within 24 hours for high risk: GRACE above 140, dynamic ECG changes.
- Selective for low risk.
- In older adults: After Eighty favoured an invasive approach at 80 and over. In SENIOR-RITA (75 and over), it reduced MI but not the overall composite. Let frailty, not age, guide the decision.

## Secondary prevention

- Dual antiplatelets for 12 months (shorter if bleeding risk is high).
- Statin, beta-blocker, ACE inhibitor.
- Blood pressure and diabetes control, stopping smoking, and cardiac rehabilitation.

## One-liner

In older adults, chest pain may be absent; consider ACS in unexplained breathlessness or confusion.
