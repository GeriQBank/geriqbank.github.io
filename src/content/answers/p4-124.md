## Agents

Dabigatran (thrombin inhibitor); rivaroxaban, apixaban and edoxaban (factor Xa inhibitors).

## Advantages over warfarin

Fixed doses, no routine INR, fewer interactions, rapid onset and offset, and **about half the intracranial bleeding**.

## Uses

- Stroke prevention in non-valvular AF.
- VTE treatment and secondary prevention.
- Prophylaxis after hip or knee replacement.
- Cancer-associated VTE (apixaban, edoxaban, rivaroxaban).
- Low-dose rivaroxaban plus aspirin in stable coronary or peripheral arterial disease (COMPASS).

## Not for

- **Mechanical valves** (harm in RE-ALIGN).
- **Rheumatic AF with moderate-to-severe mitral stenosis** (warfarin was better in INVICTUS).
- Triple-positive antiphospholipid syndrome.

## Older adults

- **The benefit holds at 75 and over** in the major trials; apixaban bleeds least in comparisons.
- **Dose reductions:**
  - Apixaban 2.5 mg twice daily if 2 of: age 80 or over, weight 60 kg or less, creatinine 1.5 mg/dL or more.
  - Rivaroxaban 15 mg if CrCl is 15–49.
  - Edoxaban 30 mg if CrCl is 15–50, weight 60 kg or less, or with interacting drugs.
  - Dabigatran 110 mg twice daily at 80 and over; avoid if CrCl is below 30.
- Use **Cockcroft–Gault**, and avoid inappropriate underdosing. Edoxaban 15 mg suits very frail octogenarians (ELDERCARE-AF).

## Monitoring

Kidney function at least yearly (more often if CrCl is below 60), blood count, adherence.

## Interactions

Strong P-gp and CYP3A4 inducers and inhibitors, notably **rifampicin (TB treatment in India)**, carbamazepine, phenytoin, azoles, ritonavir. NSAIDs and antiplatelets add bleeding.

## Reversal

Idarucizumab for dabigatran (which is also dialysable); andexanet for Xa inhibitors where available; otherwise prothrombin complex concentrate.

## Around surgery

Interrupt by bleeding risk and kidney function; no bridging needed.

## Cost

Indian generics have made them far more affordable.

## One-liner

DOACs make anticoagulation simpler and safer for older adults; dose by age, weight and Cockcroft–Gault, check the kidneys regularly, and never use them with a mechanical valve.
