## Tauopathies

- **3R and 4R mixed:** Alzheimer’s disease (tangles plus amyloid), primary age-related tauopathy, chronic traumatic encephalopathy.
- **4R:** PSP, corticobasal degeneration, argyrophilic grain disease.
- **3R:** Pick’s disease (Pick bodies).
- **MAPT mutations** (chromosome 17) cause familial FTD with parkinsonism.

## TDP-43 proteinopathies

- FTLD-TDP (types A–E).
- **ALS** (about 97% have TDP-43 inclusions).
- FTD–ALS: the **C9orf72 repeat expansion** is the commonest genetic cause of both.
- **LATE:** limbic-predominant age-related TDP-43 encephalopathy. It causes an amnestic dementia in the very old that mimics Alzheimer’s, often with hippocampal sclerosis.

## Progranulin

Loss-of-function **GRN** mutations (chromosome 17) cause haploinsufficiency and FTLD-TDP type A. Low plasma progranulin is a marker; the atrophy is asymmetric. GRN causes behavioural FTD, nonfluent aphasia or corticobasal syndrome.

## Clinical syndromes

- **Behavioural-variant FTD** (Rascovsky criteria): disinhibition, apathy, loss of empathy, compulsions, hyperorality and executive dysfunction, with memory and visuospatial skills relatively spared.
- **Primary progressive aphasia:**
  - Nonfluent/agrammatic: often tau.
  - Semantic: TDP-43 type C.
  - Logopenic: usually Alzheimer’s pathology.
- **Corticobasal syndrome:** asymmetric rigidity, apraxia, alien limb, cortical sensory loss, myoclonus.
- **PSP:** vertical gaze palsy, early falls.
- **FTD with MND:** bulbar and limb weakness, fasciculations.

## Investigations

- MRI: frontal or anterior temporal atrophy, often asymmetric, “knife-edge” gyri.
- FDG-PET; neuropsychology.
- Exclude Alzheimer’s with CSF or amyloid PET.
- Genetic testing (C9orf72, GRN, MAPT) if there’s a family history; plasma progranulin.
- EMG if MND is suspected.

## Management

- **No disease-modifying drug yet;** anti-tau, progranulin-raising and antisense therapies are in trials.
- SSRIs or trazodone for behaviour. **Cholinesterase inhibitors don’t help and may worsen FTD behaviour.**
- Cautious antipsychotics; speech therapy for aphasia; swallow and nutrition care; riluzole and MND care.
- Safety (driving, finances); genetic counselling; caregiver support; palliative care.

## One-liner

When personality or language fails before memory, think FTLD (tau, TDP-43 or progranulin underneath), and remember cholinesterase inhibitors don’t help.
