## Answer at a glance

## The need

- **Demography:** India’s older population is projected to double, from about 10% of people in 2022 to over 20% by 2050 (India Ageing Report 2023).
- **Family and social change:** nuclear families, working caregivers, isolation.
- **Health needs:** dementia and functional decline.
- **What day care adds:** it bridges home and institution, delays institutional care, and gives carers respite.

## Types

Social day care (activities, meals); medical day care or a geriatric day hospital (assessment, rehabilitation, nursing, medicines); dementia-specific day care.

## Services

- CGA and periodic review; physiotherapy and OT; exercise and falls prevention.
- Cognitive stimulation; meals and nutrition; supervised medication.
- Nursing (wound care, BP and glucose); health education; recreation; counselling.
- **Carer training and support;** transport.

## Planning

- **Groundwork:** a needs assessment and eligibility criteria.
- **Building:** an accessible ground-floor site (ramps, rails, accessible toilets, rest area, activity and dining hall, therapy room).
- **Staff:** a geriatrician lead, nurse, physiotherapist, OT, social worker, care assistants, volunteers.
- **Running it:** set hours; safety protocols (falls, emergencies, wandering); records; quality indicators.
- **Funding:** fees, CSR, NPHCE linkage, NGOs.

## Evaluate

Attendance, function, falls, carer burden (Zarit), admissions, satisfaction.

## Advice to the director

Yes. Pilot it beside the geriatric OPD or NPHCE unit with 20–30 places, linked to discharges and community services.

## One-liner

A day care centre keeps older people at home and carers sane; start small, make it accessible, and measure function, falls and carer strain.
