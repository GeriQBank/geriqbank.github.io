## Answer at a glance

1. Definition and pillars.
2. Determinants.
3. Healthy ageing and the UN Decade.
4. How to achieve it: individual, health system, community, policy and society.

## Definition (WHO, 2002)

Optimising opportunities for health, participation and security, so that quality of life improves as people age. “Active” means continued involvement in social, economic, cultural and civic life, not just physical activity.

## The pillars

Health, participation and security, with lifelong learning added later as a fourth.

## Determinants

- Gender and culture, across all the others.
- Health and social services.
- **Behaviour:** smoking, activity, diet, alcohol.
- **Personal:** biology, genetics, psychology.
- **Physical environment:** housing, falls hazards, transport, clean air and water.
- **Social:** support, education, safety from abuse.
- **Economic:** income, work, social protection.

## Healthy ageing (WHO, 2015)

Building and keeping the functional ability that enables well-being, meaning intrinsic capacity plus environment. The UN Decade of Healthy Ageing (2021–2030) focuses on age-friendly environments, combating ageism, integrated care and long-term care.

## How to achieve it

- **Individual:** activity, good diet, no tobacco, little alcohol, mental and social engagement, sleep, vaccination and screening, financial planning.
- **Health system:** WHO ICOPE screening of intrinsic capacity (cognition, mobility, nutrition, vision, hearing, mood); geriatric services; chronic disease care; rehabilitation; deprescribing.
- **Community:** WHO age-friendly cities (8 domains: outdoor spaces, transport, housing, social participation, respect, work and civic life, information, and community and health services); lifelong learning; volunteering; intergenerational programmes; day-care centres and senior citizens’ associations.
- **Policy in India:** the National Policy on Older Persons, NPHCE, the 2007 Maintenance Act, pensions, PM-JAY for everyone 70 and over, Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana, the SACRED re-employment portal, and Elderline 14567.
- **Society:** combat ageism at home, at work and in health care.

## One-liner

Active ageing isn’t just keeping busy; it’s staying healthy, involved and secure, with the environment on your side.
