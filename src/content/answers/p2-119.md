## Definition

Impaired LV relaxation and filling with raised filling pressures. HFpEF means heart failure with EF 50% or more, plus structural changes (LVH, a large left atrium, raised E/e′) and raised natriuretic peptides.

## Risk factors

Age, female sex, hypertension, obesity, diabetes, AF, CKD, coronary disease. ATTR amyloid hides in a notable minority with thick walls.

## Presentation

Exertional breathlessness, fatigue, flash pulmonary oedema with high blood pressure, and decompensation with AF.

## Diagnosis

- **Echo:** EF 50% or more; E/e′ above 14; reduced e′ (septal below 7, lateral below 10 cm/s); left atrial volume above 34 mL/m²; TR velocity above 2.8 m/s.
- NT-proBNP.
- The H₂FPEF or HFA-PEFF scores.
- Exercise echo or right heart catheter if unclear.
- Screen for amyloid red flags.

## Treatment

- SGLT2 inhibitors (fewer heart failure admissions).
- Diuretics for congestion; blood pressure control.
- Rate or rhythm control of AF; treat ischaemia.
- Exercise training and weight loss; GLP-1 agonists in obesity.
- MRA or finerenone in selected patients.
- Treat sleep apnoea, anaemia and CKD, and avoid tachycardia and dehydration.

## One-liner

For a stiff heart with normal EF, use SGLT2 inhibitors and diuretics, and control blood pressure and AF relentlessly.
