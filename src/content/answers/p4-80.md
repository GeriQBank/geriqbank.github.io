## Answer at a glance

1. Why assess nutrition.
2. The steps: screen, assess, diagnose, plan.
3. Screening tools.
4. Full assessment: the ABCDEF approach.
5. Diagnosis.

## Why

Malnutrition is common, often missed, and worsens every outcome.

## The steps

Screen → assess fully → diagnose (GLIM) → plan.

## Screening tools

- **MNA-Short Form:** out of 14; 12–14 is normal, 8–11 at risk, 0–7 malnourished.
- **Full MNA:** out of 30; 24 or more is normal, 17–23.5 at risk, below 17 malnourished.
- MUST, NRS-2002 (hospital), SNAQ, and the DETERMINE checklist (community).

## Assessment (ABCDEF)

- **A, Anthropometry:**
  - Weight and unintentional weight loss, the single most useful measure.
  - Height, estimated from knee height or arm span if the person is kyphotic.
  - BMI, mid-upper arm circumference (below 22 cm is poor), calf circumference (below 31 cm), triceps skinfold.
  - Body composition by bioimpedance or DXA; handgrip strength.
- **B, Biochemistry:**
  - Albumin and prealbumin are affected by inflammation, so they’re not diagnostic alone.
  - CRP, blood count and lymphocytes, B12, folate, iron, vitamin D, zinc, magnesium, phosphate, electrolytes, glucose, renal, liver and thyroid function.
- **C, Clinical:**
  - History: appetite, illness, drugs, teeth, swallowing, bowel symptoms, mood, cognition.
  - Examination: temporal and hand muscle wasting, fat loss, oedema, skin, hair and nails, glossitis and angular cheilitis, oral health, pressure ulcers.
  - Subjective Global Assessment (SGA).
- **D, Diet:** 24-hour recall, food frequency, 3-day food diary, and food and fluid charts in hospital.
- **E, Environment:** living alone, ability to shop and cook, finances, food insecurity, caregiver.
- **F, Function:** ADL and IADL, grip strength, gait speed.

## Diagnosis (GLIM 2019)

At least one phenotypic criterion (weight loss over 5% in 6 months or over 10% beyond 6 months; low BMI, which in Asians is below 18.5 under 70 and below 20 from 70; or low muscle mass) and one causal criterion (reduced intake or absorption, or disease-related inflammation).

## One-liner

Weight loss is the most useful nutritional sign, so always ask about it and measure it.
