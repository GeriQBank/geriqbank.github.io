## Answer at a glance

1. Who needs it.

## Who needs it

- Older adults with ESKD, whether on dialysis or choosing conservative kidney management. Their symptom burden rivals cancer.
- The “surprise question” helps identify them: would you be surprised if this patient died in the next year?

## Shared decisions

- Dialysis, conservative care, or a time-limited trial.
- For frail people over 80 with comorbidity, survival on conservative care is similar to dialysis, with more days at home (see the [CKD answer](../../paper-2/90/)).
- Advance care planning.

## Symptom control

Choose drugs that are safe in renal failure.

- **Itch:**
  - Emollients; optimise dialysis and phosphate.
  - Low-dose gabapentin or pregabalin (after dialysis); difelikefalin for haemodialysis patients; sertraline; UVB.
- **Pain:**
  - Paracetamol; no NSAIDs.
  - **Fentanyl or buprenorphine preferred;** avoid morphine, codeine and tramadol, whose metabolites accumulate.
- **Breathlessness:** fluid management, low-dose opioids, a fan, oxygen only if hypoxic.
- **Nausea and poor appetite:** low-dose haloperidol, reduced-dose metoclopramide, ondansetron.
- **Others:**
  - Fatigue; depression (sertraline); insomnia.
  - Restless legs (iron, low-dose gabapentin); cramps.

## Psychosocial and spiritual care

Psychosocial and spiritual care for the patient, and support for caregivers.

## Withdrawing dialysis

- An ethical, legitimate choice. Median survival afterwards is about 1–2 weeks.
- Manage fluid overload, myoclonus (midazolam, clonazepam), agitation and breathlessness.

## End of life

- Preferred place of death.
- Anticipatory prescribing adapted for renal failure: subcutaneous fentanyl or alfentanil, midazolam, haloperidol, glycopyrronium.
- Stop unnecessary drugs and tests.

## India

Palliative nephrology is scarce and dialysis costs push families into hardship. Integrate palliative care early (National Programme for Palliative Care; Kerala’s community model).

## One-liner

Palliative care in kidney failure isn’t giving up; it’s controlling itch, pain and breathlessness and planning ahead, whichever path is chosen.
