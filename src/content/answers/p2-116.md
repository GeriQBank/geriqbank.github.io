## Definition

A syndrome of symptoms and signs from a structural or functional heart abnormality, with raised natriuretic peptides or congestion.

**Types by ejection fraction:** HFrEF (40% or less), HFmrEF (41–49%), HFpEF (50% or more). HFpEF predominates in the very old, especially women.

## Causes

- Hypertension (the commonest in older adults), ischaemic heart disease, valve disease (AS, MR).
- Cardiomyopathies, including ATTR amyloid.
- AF, diabetes, alcohol, anaemia, thyroid disease, CKD.
- Drugs: NSAIDs, pioglitazone, verapamil or diltiazem in HFrEF.

**Precipitants:** infection, AF, ischaemia, salt, missed medicines, NSAIDs.

## Clinical features

- Breathlessness, orthopnoea, PND, oedema, fatigue.
- In the very old, often atypical: confusion, falls, reduced activity, anorexia, night cough.
- Signs: raised JVP, S3, crackles, oedema.

## Investigations

- NT-proBNP (acute thresholds rise with age, for example 1,800 pg/mL above 75).
- ECG, echo, chest X-ray.
- Blood count, renal function, electrolytes, liver function, TSH.
- Iron studies: iron deficiency is ferritin below 100, or 100–299 with TSAT below 20%.

## HFrEF: the four pillars

- ARNI (or ACE inhibitor or ARB).
- Beta-blocker (bisoprolol, carvedilol, metoprolol succinate, or nebivolol, which was studied in the elderly in SENIORS).
- MRA.
- SGLT2 inhibitor.

Plus loop diuretics for congestion, IV iron for deficiency, and devices individualised.

## HFpEF

- SGLT2 inhibitors (EMPEROR-Preserved, DELIVER), and finerenone (FINEARTS-HF).
- Diuretics for congestion.
- Control blood pressure, AF and ischaemia.
- Semaglutide for obesity-related HFpEF.

## Non-drug care

Moderate salt restriction, daily weights, cardiac rehabilitation, vaccines, no NSAIDs.

## In an 85-year-old lady

- Do a comprehensive geriatric assessment: frailty, cognition, polypharmacy, orthostatic hypotension, kidneys, potassium.
- Start low and add one drug at a time.
- Avoid over-diuresis, which causes falls and AKI. Use digoxin only at low dose with levels checked.
- A heart failure nurse and home monitoring help; discuss goals of care, and involve palliative care in advanced disease.

## One-liner

In the very old, heart failure is usually HFpEF, and the art lies in balancing congestion against dizziness and the kidneys.
